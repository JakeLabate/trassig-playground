Installation

Three ways to get it installed

After a couple of decades in the business we designed our own installation and maintenance products, then built three install paths around them so the labor matches the size of the job.

  1. Do it yourself, with an install care specialist

    For site amenities, small sets, repair kits, and small surfacing areas. We assign you an install care specialist you can call with questions before or on the day of the install, and we provide detailed manuals and how to videos.

  2. Your handyman, with an install care specialist

    You find a local, trusted, insured tradesperson with the right skill set. Poured in surface repair, for example, can be done by landscapers, stone masons, or anyone who has finished concrete. A Trassig install care specialist is assigned to your installer and assists directly by phone or video.

  3. A qualified installation company

    For larger jobs we pair you with a local qualified installer, ship all the materials directly to you, and they perform the install.
