Playground maintenance, surfacing, and safety inspections

Three things we do

Fix it, surface it, or have it inspected

Most playground problems fall into one of three buckets. Trassig covers all three, and the same people answer the phone for each.

Maintenance and repair products

Repair kits for poured in place rubber, synthetic turf, playground decking, and running tracks, plus the binders, conditioners, and tools that go with them.

  • Poured in place rubber repair kits
  • Rebinder and Extender surface conditioners
  • Playground deck repair putty
  • Synthetic turf and running track kits

Safety surfacing and equipment

Full surfacing systems and commercial play equipment, sold to schools, parks, daycares, and property managers, with site amenities and sports equipment alongside.

  • Poured in place rubber, turf, tiles, mulch
  • Composite playsets by age group
  • Swings, climbers, spring riders, panels
  • Shade structures, benches, bike racks

Risk management

Certified playground inspections and ASTM audits across New England and the mid Atlantic, plus remote code and ADA compliance review anywhere in the world.

  • CPSI inspections to ASTM and CPSC
  • Surface impact testing (HIC and GMAX)
  • Remote playground code audits
  • ADA compliance review

Why the phone call matters

Everyone here is an installer first

Every person who works at Trassig is a seasoned installer as well as a certified playground inspector who understands the ASTM and CPSC codes that govern playground equipment and safety surfacing.

That is the whole reason the do it yourself products work. When you buy a repair kit, you are paired with an install care specialist you can call before or during the job, and it costs nothing extra. We have already made the mistakes we do not want you to make.

  • Free install guidance by phone or video with every maintenance product
  • Detailed installation manuals and how to videos with each kit
  • Stocked products typically ship the same day when ordered before noon Eastern
  • Warehouse pickup available in Bethel if you want to skip shipping
