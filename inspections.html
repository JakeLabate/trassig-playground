What an inspection is

A certified inspector, gauges and probes, and a written record

An experienced certified playground inspector conducts a thorough inspection of all playground equipment and safety surfacing to ASTM and CPSC standards, using the gauges and probes those standards specify.

If there are code issues you receive a written report with pictures listing every hazard, ranked by priority. If there are no hazards you get a compliance letter for your records and for any agency that asks for it.

The findings are shared with you only. We will not share your report with anyone else without your permission. If an agency disputes a prior conflicting report, they can call us directly and we will explain the findings.

The report

Every hazard is ranked one through five

The priority ranking is the working part of the report. It tells your maintenance team what to fix today and what to watch, so a limited budget goes to the right thing first.

  1. Correct immediately

    A non compliant safety concern that may result in permanent disability, loss of life, or loss of a body part.

  2. Correct as soon as possible

    A non compliant safety concern that may result in temporary disability.

  3. Correct when time permits

    A non compliant safety concern likely to cause a minor, non disabling injury.

  4. Correct if it worsens

    A non compliant safety concern whose potential to cause an injury is very minimal.

  5. Compliant, keep maintaining

    The item is compliant with the owner or operator standard of care. Ongoing preventive maintenance is recommended.

The process

How a playground safety inspection is done

  1. Send photos and the address

    Email pictures and the address of your play area to info@trassig.com and we send you a quote.

  2. Approve and schedule

    Once the quote is approved we schedule the inspection with you.

  3. The site inspection

    A certified playground inspector arrives and inspects the equipment using the probes and measurements outlined by CPSC and ASTM.

  4. The written report

    A detailed report is sent to you in PDF, prioritizing all hazards found, with pictures showing each one.

  5. The follow up call

    The inspector walks you through how to remedy what was found, and flags items that are compliant now but worth watching.
